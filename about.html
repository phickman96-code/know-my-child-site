Why we built it

The story behind Know My Child.

From a school psychologist who watched the gap in care, every single day — and a parent who decided to close it.

I've spent my career as a school psychologist protecting and advocating for children — especially those with special needs. In schools, we build systems around them: data, documentation, communication, and intervention plans. When something is off, we catch it. We respond. We support.

But I kept seeing a different reality outside of school.

When a parent drops their child off at a church daycare, a gym childcare center, or with a babysitter — those systems disappear. There's no structured documentation. No consistent communication. No shared understanding of what a child needs in the moment.

And that gap matters. Because the same level of awareness, communication, and early intervention that helps children succeed in school is often missing in the everyday environments where they spend so much of their time.

The other gap most people don't see

Even inside schools and daycares, parents are often on the outside looking in — receiving delayed updates, limited context, and little ability to actively shape what's happening with their child in real time.

As both a psychologist and a parent, I couldn't ignore that.

Know My Child was created to do more than extend support beyond school. It was built to put the power back into the hands of parents.

What changes when information flows in real time

Through real-time documentation and communication, parents and caregivers can share critical insights the moment they happen — what a child experienced, what triggered a behavior, what helped them regulate, what they need next. Educators and caregivers no longer work in isolation. They work with clarity.

And that changes everything. Because when information flows in real time, support becomes proactive — not reactive.

It also goes beyond the classroom

The same circle of care that supports a child at school can now help parents plan everyday life. Whether it's a trip to the zoo, a visit to a museum, or a new environment, the app provides guidance — what to prepare for, what to bring, what to avoid, and how to set the child up for success based on their unique needs and patterns.

This isn't just communication. It's continuous understanding.

It's a system that connects school, home, and the real world — so every adult in a child's life is informed, aligned, and equipped to support them in the moment and plan for what comes next.

Because children don't only need support at school. They need to be understood — everywhere they go.

Dr. Philip Van Hickman, Ed.D., Ed.S., NCSP Founder · MindAble Solutions LLC · Nationally Certified School Psychologist
