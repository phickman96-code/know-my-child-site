Why we built it

From a school psychologist who watched the gap, every day.

I’ve spent my career as a school psychologist protecting and advocating for children — especially those with special needs. When something is off at school, we catch it, we respond, we support. But the moment a parent drops their child off somewhere else, that scaffolding disappears.

And even inside schools and clinics, parents are often on the outside looking in — delayed updates, limited context, little ability to shape what’s happening in real time.

Know My Child was built to put that power back in parents’ hands. Capture the day the moment it happens — by voice, in seconds — and the understanding travels with your child: what they experienced, what set them off, what helped, what they need next.

When information flows in real time, support becomes proactive instead of reactive. Because children don’t only need support at school. They need to be understood — everywhere they go.

Dr. Philip William Van Hickman Founder, MindAble Solutions LLC · Nationally Certified School Psychologist
