Frequently Asked Questions

How do I share my child's profile with a caregiver?

Go to the Sharing tab, tap the + button, and enter the caregiver's name and role. You can choose which sections to share and whether to include them in your Circle. They'll receive an invite link to create their caregiver account.

Can I control what information caregivers see?

Yes. You have full control over sharing. You decide which caregivers have access and can revoke access at any time from the Sharing tab.

Is Ask the Circle a replacement for medical advice?

No. Ask the Circle provides informational guidance based on your child's profile. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult qualified health providers for medical questions.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your iPhone, find Know My Child, and tap Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

Contact us at support@knowmychild.app to request account deletion. All personal data and child profile data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Is my child's data safe?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use row-level security to ensure only authorized users can access your data. You control all sharing, and no data is sold to third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
